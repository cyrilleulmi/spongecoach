# Acht

- **Photos:** 2, half-rink side, rotated: the goal is on the left, the centre line on the right.
- **Tags:** Schiessen, Passen
- **Photo relation:** continuation — photo 2 mirrors photo 1, so the two sides alternate one
  after the other (the figure eight). One Stage.

## Stage 1

- Actors (all one side, neutral): queues of two in each corner: Q1 top-left and Q2 bottom-left
  (the corners next to the goal line), Q3 top-right and Q4 bottom-right (the corners at the
  centre line).
- Steps, photo 1:
  1. The first player of Q3 runs diagonally to the slot.
  2. The first player of Q2 passes to the runner in the slot.
  3. The runner shoots at the goal.
- Steps, photo 2 (mirrored), starting after the shot in step 3:
  4. The first player of Q4 runs diagonally to the slot.
  5. The first player of Q1 passes to the runner.
  6. The runner shoots.

## Must ask

- Whether the photos are progression or continuation (unless selected on upload).

## May assume

- No goalie (none drawn, so it doesn't matter; animate an empty goal).
- The pass starts as the runner reaches the slot.
- Repetition: a seamless loop may be guessed (queue drill) or asked.

## Coach answers

- Loop: seamless. The next player in each queue steps up. Where the shooter and the passer go
  isn't drawn: accept any rotation the interpreter proposes that keeps all four queues filled.
