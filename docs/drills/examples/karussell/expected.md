# Karussell

- **Photos:** 1, full-rink side, portrait. Drill takes place in the bottom half.
- **Tags:** Schiessen, Passen, Wiederholung spiegelverkehrt
- **Photo relation:** single photo, one Stage. (The coach may draw this drill across many photos
  as a continuation.)

## Stage 1

- Actors (neutral): queue L of three in the bottom-left corner, queue R of three in the
  bottom-right corner. The first player of R has the ball.
- Steps:
  1. The first player of L runs a wide arc up towards the centre circle and then down to the
     high slot.
  2. The first player of R passes up to the runner.
  3. The runner shoots at the bottom goal.
- Repetition, mirrored, seamless: the next run starts from R with an arc on the right side and a
  pass from L; the loop keeps alternating sides.

## Must ask

- Nothing essential.

## May assume

- No goalie (none drawn, so it doesn't matter).
- The pass is timed to meet the runner at the end of the arc.

## Coach answers

- Loop: mirrored and seamless. Where the shooter and the passer go isn't drawn: accept any
  rotation the interpreter proposes that keeps both queues filled.
