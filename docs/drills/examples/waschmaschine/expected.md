## Stage 1

- Actors (neutral):
  - C in the slot, at the top edge of the goal area.
  - BL: a queue of three in the bottom-left corner (next to the goal line); the first player has
    the ball.
  - BR in the bottom-right corner, TR in the top-right corner (both at the centre line).
  - TL in the top-left corner (next to the goal line).
- Steps (numbered 1–11; passes have arrowheads). The ball goes round the rink through C, and
  every passer then moves one corner on (BL → BR → TR → TL):

  | # | Step |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | BL passes to C |
  | – | BL runs along the bottom boards to the bottom middle |
  | 2 | C passes to BL at the bottom middle |
  | 3 | BL passes to BR, then runs on (curving along the boards) into the bottom-right corner |
  | 4 | BR passes to C |
  | – | BR runs up the right side to the centre-line middle |
  | 5 | C passes to BR at the centre-line middle |
  | 6 | BR passes up to TR, then runs on into the top-right corner |
  | 7 | TR passes to C |
  | – | TR runs along the top boards to the top middle |
  | 8 | C passes to TR at the top middle |
  | 9 | TR passes to TL, then runs on (curving) into the top-left corner |
  | 10 | TL passes to C |
  | 11 | C shoots at the goal |

## Must ask

- Replay or seamless loop. The drawing doesn't show how one repetition leads into the next.
  Suggest the seamless version with a proposal for who goes where.

## May assume

- No goalie (none drawn, so it doesn't matter).
- Who runs which solid line follows from the ball: whoever just passed runs on to where the next
  pass arrives.
- Each run starts right after that player's pass, and the return pass is timed to meet them.

## Coach answers

- Loop: seamless. After the shot, C moves out to where the first pass came from (the BL corner)
  and TL, the last player to pass, moves into the middle and becomes the new C. The next BL
  player starts the next repetition.
