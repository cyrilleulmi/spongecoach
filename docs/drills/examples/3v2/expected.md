# 3v2

- **Photos:** 1, full-rink side, portrait. Drill takes place in the top half.
- **Tags:** Mit Gegenspielern, Überzahl, Passen
- **Photo relation:** single photo, one Stage.
- **Kind:** a game situation with no fixed order. The animation shows the *idea* (keep the ball
  3 v 2), not an exact sequence.

## Stage 1

- Actors:
  - Side A (blue X): A1 on the left side, A2 on the right side, A3 at the centre line.
  - Side B (red O): D1 left of centre, D2 right of centre.
- Steps, after the coach confirms (see Must ask):
  - A1, A2 and A3 roam around their positions (the curved double-headed arrows) and pass among
    themselves in an order the interpreter makes up. Passes go along the drawn lanes (A1 ↔ A3,
    A2 ↔ A3) and may go straight between the wings.
  - D1 and D2 move to follow the ball and cover passing lanes. They never intercept.
  - About 6–8 passes, then the loop starts again.

## Must ask

- What the O are (defenders or cones), unless the tag "Mit Gegenspielern" is set.
- That this is a game situation without a fixed order. The question must come with a concrete
  proposal, for example: "Should the attackers pass among themselves in a random order while the
  defenders just move around?" Only after the coach confirms does the interpreter make up an
  order.

## May assume

- The exact pass order and roam paths, once confirmed.
- No goalie, and no shot (none drawn).

## Coach answers

- Ja, es ist eine Spielsituation: Die Angreiferinnen passen in zufälliger Reihenfolge untereinander,
  die Verteidigerinnen bewegen sich mit. Kein Abschluss.
