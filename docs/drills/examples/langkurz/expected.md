# Langkurz

- **Photos:** 1, full-rink side, portrait. Drill takes place in the top half, above the centre
  circle.
- **Tags:** Passen, Wiederholung spiegelverkehrt
- **Photo relation:** single photo, one Stage.

## Stage 1

- Actors (neutral): L, a queue of two at the left boards (the first player has the ball); R, one
  player at the right boards.
- Steps (long pass, then a short one-two):
  1. L plays a long pass across to R (top dashed line, arrowhead at R).
  2. L runs towards the middle (solid line).
  3. R plays a short pass to L in the middle (lower dashed line, arrowhead towards the middle).
  4. L passes back to R (same line, arrowhead at R).
- Repetition, mirrored ("Wiederholung spiegelverkehrt"): seamless.
  - L goes to the right side and waits for her turn.
  - R now plays L's part from the right: a long pass to the next L player, a run to the middle,
    and a one-two with her.
  - The steps are mirrored across the rink's long axis (left ↔ right). The loop keeps alternating
    sides.

## Must ask

- Nothing. The arrowheads and the ball settle steps 3 and 4 (R has the ball after the long pass,
  so R passes first), and the tag settles the repetition.

## May assume

- The runner stops in the middle to play the return pass.

## Coach answers

- Nach dem Rückpass geht L auf die rechte Seite und wartet, bis sie wieder dran ist. R spielt den
  nächsten Durchgang spiegelverkehrt von rechts.
