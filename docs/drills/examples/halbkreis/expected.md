# Halbkreis

- **Photos:** 1, full-rink side, portrait. Drill takes place in the bottom half.
- **Tags:** Schiessen, Mit Goalie
- **Photo relation:** single photo, one Stage.

## Stage 1

- Actors: five shooters S1–S5 in a half circle between the centre line and the bottom goal area,
  from left to right; goalie G (bottom goal).
- Steps (numbered 1–5 on the photo):
  1. S1 (far left) shoots.
  2. S2 shoots.
  3. S3 (centre) shoots.
  4. S4 shoots.
  5. S5 (far right) shoots.

## Must ask

- Nothing essential. The numbers give the order; the double lines are shots.

## May assume

- Each shooter has their own ball.
- The gap between shots (about 2 s).
- Shooters shoot from where they stand.
