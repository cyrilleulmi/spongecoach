# Slalom

- **Photos:** 1, full-rink side, portrait.
- **Tags:** Passen
- **Photo relation:** single photo, one Stage.

## Stage 1

- Actors (neutral): queue Q of three in the bottom-right corner; the first player starts with the
  ball. Props: four cones (red squares) in a line along the right side, from the bottom half to
  the top half.
- Steps:
  1. The first player of Q runs with the ball in a slalom through the cones up the right side
     (solid line: a run carrying the ball, not a dribble).
  2. They turn behind the top face-off cross and run back down with the ball, left of the cones,
     to the middle of the top half.
  3. They pass (dashed) back down to the next player in Q.

## Must ask

- Nothing essential.

## May assume

- Running speed through the cones.
- Who has the ball follows from the pass at the end: the runner carries it the whole way.
- Repetition: a seamless loop may be guessed (queue drill) or asked.

## Coach answers

- Loop: seamless. The passer joins the back of Q, and the player who received the pass starts
  the next run.
