# Bresil

- **Photos:** 4, full-rink side, portrait.
- **Tags:** Mit Gegenspielern, Mit Goalie, Überzahl, Schiessen
- **Photo relation:** progression — each photo is its own Stage (1 v G → 1 v 1 → 2 v 1 → 3 v 2).
- **Note:** marker colours swap between photos (blue attacks in 1 and 3, red in 2 and 4). Colour
  says nothing about side across photos.

## Stage 1 — photo 1: 1 v Goalie

- Actors: attacker A1 (in front of the top goal area), goalie G (bottom goal).
- Steps:
  1. A1 dribbles straight down through the centre circle past the centre line.
  2. A1 shoots at the bottom goal.

## Stage 2 — photo 2: 1 v 1

- Actors: attacker A1 (in front of the top goal area), defender D1 (bottom half, centre), goalie G.
- Steps:
  1. A1 dribbles down through the centre, veering slightly right after the centre line.
  2. D1 roams side to side while A1 approaches (double-headed arrow).
  3. A1 shoots from the right of the slot at the bottom goal.

## Stage 3 — photo 3: 2 v 1

- Actors: attackers A1 (top left), A2 (top right), defender D1 (bottom half, centre), goalie G.
- Steps:
  1. A1 dribbles down the left side to the centre line; at the same time A2 runs down the right side.
  2. A1 passes across to A2 just below the centre line.
  3. D1 roams side to side.
  4. A2 shoots at the bottom goal.

## Stage 4 — photo 4: 3 v 2

- Actors: attackers A1 (top left), A2 (top right), A3 (bottom half, left of the slot), defenders
  D1, D2 (just below the centre line, left and right), goalie G.
- Steps:
  1. A2 dribbles down the right side; A1 runs down the left side to the centre line.
  2. A2 passes across (curved over the centre circle) to A1.
  3. A1 passes diagonally down to the right, towards the slot.
  4. A3 runs right to receive it.
  5. D1, D2 roam side to side.
  6. A3 shoots at the bottom goal.

## Must ask

- Nothing essential, if the tags are set. Without "Mit Gegenspielern", ask which side each X is on
  in photos 2–4.

## May assume

- Defender movement inside the double-headed arrows (roam).
- Speeds, and the timing between runs and passes.

- The attacking goal and the goalie are the same in all four Stages.
